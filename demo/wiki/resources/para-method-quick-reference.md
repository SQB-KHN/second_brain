# PARA Method (quick reference)

- **Projects** — active work with a deadline
- **Areas** — ongoing responsibilities to maintain
- **Resources** — topics of interest for future use
- **Archives** — inactive items from the other three

SecondSelf auto-classifies every capture into one of these buckets.
