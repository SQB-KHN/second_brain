# Professional Skills for Claude

Skills that do the mechanical half of professional work, so you keep the half that needs judgement.

Each one installs into your own Claude account and runs on files you export yourself. Nothing to install on your machine, nothing to connect, no coding.

---

## What a skill actually is

A skill is a set of instructions you upload to Claude once. After that Claude follows it whenever your work calls for it, without being asked again.

That makes it different from a prompt in three ways that matter. A prompt is something you retype and reword every time. A skill carries the domain judgement a senior professional would apply, so it knows that a cost saving is favourable and a revenue shortfall is not. And it knows what to verify before handing you an answer, so a variance table that does not sum to the total variance gets reported as broken rather than presented as finished.

## Who these are for

Working professionals who do the same demanding task every week or month, and are judged on getting it right.

Not developers, and no technical background is assumed. If your week involves exporting a file, wrestling it into shape, and explaining the result to somebody more senior than you, these are built for that.

The defaults are India-first. Indian digit grouping, lakh and crore, GST and TDS handling, Tally and Zoho and Busy exports, IST timezone traps. They work elsewhere without complaint.

## Installing a skill

**First, get the files.** GitHub does not offer per-folder downloads, so pick whichever suits you:

- Click **Code**, then **Download ZIP**, and take the skill folders you want, or
- Open the `SKILL.md` you want, click **Raw**, and save the page as `SKILL.md`

**Then install it:**

1. On claude.ai, open **Settings**, go to **Capabilities**, and turn on **code execution and file creation**. Most of these skills run Python on your file and will not work without it. The guide for each skill says whether it needs this.
2. Put `SKILL.md` in a folder named exactly as the skill folder here, for example `finance-reconciliation`. The guide does not need to go in.
3. Compress that folder to a `.zip`.
4. Go to **Customize**, then **Skills**, then add a skill and upload the zip.

Repeat for each skill you want. Skills you upload stay private to your account. Once installed they work in Chat and Cowork, and Claude picks the right one from what you ask, so you do not have to name it.

**Do not rename `SKILL.md`.** That is the filename Claude looks for, and renaming it stops the skill loading.

### If you are on a free plan

Thirty of the fifty-five need no code execution at all: the Finance Leadership Brief, SQL Writer, Metric Definition, Positioning Brief, Campaign Plan, Operations Review Brief, the three document-based Healthcare skills (Clinical Documentation, Claim Readiness, Patient Instructions), four of the five Content skills (everything except the Performance Audit), three of the five Sales skills (Deal Qualification, Discovery and Debrief, Forecast Commit), four of the five Educators skills (everything except Item Analysis), four of the five Techies skills (everything except the Postmortem), three of the five Business Owners skills (Contract Review, Quotation, Difficult Conversations), and three of the five Students skills (Understanding Check, Answer Writing, Study Plan). Customer Language Synthesis and the Funnel Audit work without it too, and only use it when you supply numbers. The rest read and compute on your files, so they need it.

## What makes these different from asking Claude directly

**They expect real exports, not clean data.** Group and subtotal rows that double count if you sum them. Debit and credit split across two columns. Brackets for negatives. Digit grouping like 12,34,567. Headers starting on row 6. Dates that read two ways. Invoice references formatted differently in every system. This is where generic AI output falls over on the first real file.

**They compute in code, never in prose.** Every number is produced by running Python on your file. Nothing arithmetic is worked out inside a sentence, because that is the single largest source of error in AI output.

**They prove the numbers tie, and show you.** Line variances must sum to the total. Matched plus unmatched must equal total rows. A trial balance must balance before a pack is built. Every check is reported whether it passes or fails, and a failure is stated at the top, never buried inside a file described as complete.

**They never lose anything quietly.** Removed rows go to a visible quarantine. Accounts that cannot be mapped appear in an Unmapped section with their balance. Excluded values are stated with their amount. Silent loss looks finished and is wrong.

**Assumptions are labelled next to the number they affect.** An assumed collection rate or conversion rate says so, in place. You always know what the output is resting on, and an assumption is never presented as a benchmark or a finding.

**Evidence and interpretation stay apart.** What the data shows is stated as fact. What might explain it is labelled a hypothesis, with the check that would confirm it and the person who could answer. No causal claim is made from a single dataset.

**Nothing is invented to fill a gap.** No fabricated numbers, quotes, proof points, benchmarks or reasons. A missing input becomes a stated assumption or an open question.

**They say when the answer is inconclusive**, in the first paragraph rather than buried at the end. "This data cannot settle that" is often the correct finding, and it protects you from presenting something that unravels in the room.

**Personal data gets flagged once.** Where a skill's input routinely carries names, emails, phone numbers or customer identifiers, it says so once, asks whether you are permitted to share the file, and offers to work with those fields removed. Then it continues with whatever you decide. It does not lecture and it does not repeat.

**Healthcare and Educators go further.** Those inputs carry patient and student identifiers, and the Educators inputs are about children, so de-identified is the default rather than the option. Each of those skills names the identifying fields first and none needs an identity to work, so identifiers are held as placeholders, or roll numbers, that you reinstate yourself.

## What these will not do

- **No system access.** They work on files you export and text you paste. They never connect to your accounting system, CRM, ad account, database, bank or any government portal.
- **No regulated advice.** No tax, GST or statutory positions. No accounting treatment or provisioning decisions. No legal clearance. Skills compute, check, structure and flag; the judgement stays with you.
- **No invented evidence.** No fabricated statistics, testimonials, market positions or promised lift percentages.
- **No causal claims from data alone.** They show where a change is concentrated and what is consistent with it, not what caused it.
- **Nothing is posted, sent, filed or changed.** Every output is for a human to review and act on.

## What you need

- A Claude account, with code execution and file creation turned on for most of these
- Your files in Excel or CSV, and text pasted in
- Nothing else. No installation, no terminal, no coding, nothing to connect

## Repository layout

```
<domain>-skills/
├── README.md                       ← the domain page: five skills, install, boundaries
└── <domain>-<skill>/
    ├── SKILL.md                    ← the skill itself, this is what you upload
    └── <domain>-<skill>-guide.md   ← how to use it, inputs, limits, troubleshooting
```

Every skill folder carries two files. `SKILL.md` is the skill and goes into the zip you upload. The guide beside it is for you to read, and covers the exact inputs it needs, the phrasings that trigger it, what good output looks like, its limits, and a troubleshooting table.

Skill folder names are prefixed with the domain, because a skill name has to be unique inside a Claude account.
